# Cold Call Script — Web Design Pitch
## For businesses with NO website (phone-only leads)

---

### Opening (10 seconds)
> "Hi, is this [Business Name]? Great — my name's John, I'm a web designer based in the Boise area. I'm reaching out because I noticed your business doesn't have a website, and I wanted to see if that's something you've been thinking about."

---

### If they say yes / sounds interested:
> "I build websites for local businesses — pretty simple process, usually done in about a week. Starting at $75 for a basic site. I can show you a free mockup first so you can see exactly what it would look like before paying anything.

> What's the best email to send that to?"

---

### If they say they're not interested / have a Facebook page:
> "Totally understand. Facebook's great, but a lot of people Google '[your service] near me' and if there's no website, you just don't show up. Even a simple one-pager with your hours and phone number makes a big difference.

> I can put together a free mockup — takes me maybe an hour — and you can decide if it's worth it. No obligation. Want me to send it over?"

---

### If they ask about price:
> "Depends on what you need. One page with contact info, hours, and what you do — $75. Three pages with photos and more detail — $150. Larger sites with everything — $250. All delivered in under a week."

---

### Closing:
> "Great — what's your email? I'll send over a mockup and you can tell me what you think."

---
