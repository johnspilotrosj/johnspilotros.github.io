Who We Are

Dry Creek Services is a locally owned home services company based in Eagle, Idaho, serving the Treasure Valley. We provide dryer vent cleaning, landscaping, junk removal, and power washing. You can reach us at (208) 985-5394 or johnspilotros@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request through our contact form, we collect the following information you voluntarily provide:

  • First and last name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Service address and job details

We do not collect payment information through this website. We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or behavioral tracking tools beyond what is inherent to loading third-party fonts (Google Fonts) in your browser.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Contact you to discuss your service request and provide a quote
  • Schedule and confirm service appointments
  • Follow up after service if needed

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties for marketing purposes.

Form Submission Processing

Quote request forms on this site are processed through Formspree, a third-party form handling service. By submitting a form, your data is transmitted to Formspree's servers before being forwarded to us. Formspree's own privacy policy governs their handling of that data during transmission.

Data Retention

We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to fulfill your service request or respond to your inquiry. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at (208) 985-5394 or johnspilotros@gmail.com.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted to us through this website.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of this website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
