Our Story

A Salon Built on
the Details

Soleil opened in 2016 because we believed Tacoma deserved a salon that felt like a real retreat — not just a haircut factory. We keep our team small, our standards high, and our schedule personal.

Every stylist and therapist at Soleil has been hand-selected for both their technical skill and how they make clients feel. That's why 80% of our clients are repeat guests.

  • Family-owned and operated since 2016
  • Licensed in Washington State for all services
  • Continued education — our team stays current
  • Clean, relaxing environment — always
