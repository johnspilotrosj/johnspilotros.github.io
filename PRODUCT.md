# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Home buyers and sellers in Boise and the wider Treasure Valley (Meridian, Eagle, Nampa, Star, Kuna): first-time buyers, growing families, downsizers, and out-of-state relocators moving to Idaho. They are usually browsing on a phone in the evening, comparing a few agents, and deciding who to trust with the biggest transaction of their lives. They want a real, responsive local person, not a corporate machine or a lead-farm.

The site owner is **John Spilotros**, a licensed Idaho real estate **salesperson** (not a broker, not an appraiser) affiliated with **Keller Williams Boise**. The job the site does for him: establish credibility, capture buyer and seller leads, and clearly communicate how he works.

## Product Purpose

A four-page marketing site (Home, About, Buyers & Sellers, Contact) that:
- Builds trust quickly with an honest, local, premium feel.
- Generates leads through a contact form and a free home-value (CMA) request.
- Gives buyers a useful interactive mortgage estimator and sellers a CMA request flow.
- Stays fully compliant with Idaho Real Estate Commission (IREC) advertising rules and the federal Fair Housing Act.

Success = a visitor feels "this person is local, straight with me, and easy to reach," and they call or submit a form.

## Brand Personality

Grounded, honest, and quietly premium. Three words: **rooted, straightforward, warm**. The voice is a real person talking plainly ("a real person who picks up the phone"), never salesy or corporate. It should feel like the Treasure Valley itself: high-desert and foothills, unpretentious but with genuine quality.

## Anti-references

- **The navy-and-gold "luxury realtor" cliché.** The single most overused look in real estate. Avoided deliberately (evergreen + foothills clay instead).
- **Lead-farm / portal aesthetics** (Zillow-style listing grids, aggressive pop-ups, fake urgency).
- **Fabricated trust signals.** No invented testimonials, sales stats, or listings — these are false advertising for a real licensee. Honest content only.
- **AI-template tells:** eyebrow kickers above every section, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, hero-metric templates, em-dash-heavy copy.
- **Editorial-magazine affectation** (display-serif italics + mono labels + broadsheet grid) — this is a property brand, not a magazine.

## Design Principles

1. **Honest by construction.** Every claim on the site must be true and verifiable. Placeholders are clearly marked rather than filled with plausible fiction. Compliance is a design constraint, not an afterthought.
2. **Local is the luxury.** The brand's premium feel comes from place (foothills, pine, the valley), typography, and restraint — not from gold trim or stock opulence.
3. **A real person, reachable.** Warmth and responsiveness are the core promise; the design keeps "talk to John" one tap away on every screen.
4. **Useful beats flashy.** Interactive pieces (mortgage estimator, CMA request, neighborhood explorer, instant cached navigation) earn their place by helping the visitor, not by decorating.
5. **Calm confidence.** Generous space, one committed color, no shouting. Trust is built by looking settled and well-made.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥ 3:1, visible focus states, semantic HTML, keyboard-operable controls (the neighborhood explorer supports arrow keys; forms use native validation).
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support: reveals, view-transitions, and entrance animations all collapse to instant/crossfade.
- Fair Housing is both legal and inclusive: Equal Housing Opportunity logo + statement, and no language expressing a preference or limitation based on any protected class.
- Alt text on all imagery; no information conveyed by color alone.
