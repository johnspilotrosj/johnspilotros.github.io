# Ready-to-Post Listings

**Before your first post, do one find-and-replace across this file:**

| Placeholder | Replace with |
|---|---|
| `Dry Creek Services` | Your business name |
| `(215) 859-3267` | The number you want calls/texts on |
| `the Treasure Valley` | Your main city/county (e.g. "Greater Springfield & Hamilton County") |
| `[FB]` | Link to your Facebook profile or page |

Settings baked into this copy: service area = whole metro/county · no prices shown (free quote) · customers can **call/text OR message**.

> **Facebook Marketplace price field:** Marketplace forces a number. Type `0` — it will display as "Free", which is fine and gets clicks; the body text makes clear it's a free *quote*, not free work. (Alternatively post these in local Facebook community / Buy-Sell-Trade groups, which have no price field.)
