## OPTIONAL — paid lead marketplaces (these COST money — your call)

These send you leads but charge you, either per-lead or per-month. Only worth it once the free channels are running and you want more volume. Costs are why they're separate:

- **Thumbtack** — you pay per lead you respond to (~$8–$40 depending on service/area).
- **Angi / HomeAdvisor** — monthly + per-lead. More expensive; read terms carefully.
- **Bark** — you buy "credits" to contact leads.

Profile/bio copy for all three is in `PROFILES.md` if you decide to try them. My recommendation: run the free stuff for 2–3 weeks first, see from `customers.xlsx` how many jobs you're getting and at what value, *then* decide if paid leads are worth it.
