# The Run-It-Yourself Marketing Playbook

This is built to take **~15 minutes a week**. Follow it as-is.

---

## Where to post (all free, ranked by payoff)

1. **Google Business Profile** — *Do this first, this week.* Set up a free profile at google.com/business. Local-service searches ("dryer vent cleaning near me") and Google Maps drive more steady calls than anything else, and it's free forever. Add the 4 services, your area, phone, and 5+ photos of finished jobs.
2. **Facebook Marketplace** — post each service as its own listing (copy in `LISTINGS.md`).
3. **Facebook local groups** — search for "[your town] community", "[county] buy sell trade", "[town] recommendations" groups. Join 5–10. These have no price field and high trust.
4. **Nextdoor** — one business/recommendation post (copy in `LISTINGS.md`). Very strong for home services.
5. **Craigslist** — one combined ad under Services (copy in `LISTINGS.md`).
6. **OfferUp** — mirror the Marketplace listings; takes 2 minutes once written.

---

## The weekly routine (every Monday, ~15 min)

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Renew Marketplace listings.** FB lets you "Renew" or delete + repost. Repost each of the 4. |
| 2 | **Rotate the title.** Switch to the *other* title option in `LISTINGS.md`. Identical reposts get down-ranked — changing the title + first line resets that. |
| 3 | **Bump one Facebook group post.** Post one service into one local group (rotate which group/service each week so you're not spamming any single group). |
| 4 | **Add this week's photos** to Google Business Profile. Even one before/after shot keeps you ranking. |
| 5 | **Log every lead** in `customers.xlsx` the moment it comes in (see below). |

Set a recurring phone reminder for Monday 8am: "Marketing 15 min."

---

## Don't get your Facebook account banned

- **Never** use auto-poster / bot software for Marketplace. Instant permanent ban. Manual reposting only.
- Don't post the **same word-for-word text** repeatedly — that's why we rotate titles and first lines.
- Don't post the same service into 10 groups the same day. Spread across the week.
- One listing per service, not duplicates of the same service stacked up.
- Reply to messages within a few hours — fast response is rewarded by FB and by customers.

---

## When a customer messages or calls — close it fast

Speed wins these jobs. First responder usually gets the work. Reply template:

> Hey [name], thanks for reaching out! I cover [your area] and can take a look. To give you a quick quote — what's the address (or general area), and can you send a photo of the [vent / yard / pile / surface]? I can usually get out within a few days.

Then **immediately** open `customers.xlsx` → `Leads & Jobs` tab and add a row:
- **Date / Time** they contacted
- **Service** (dropdown)
- **Customer Name + Phone/Contact**
- **Address / Area**
- **Source** (dropdown — be honest about which platform; after a month the `Summary` tab tells you which platform actually pays, so you double down there)
- **Status** = `New Lead`, then update it (`Quoted` → `Scheduled` → `Completed` → `Paid`) as it moves

The `Summary` tab adds up leads, completed jobs, and collected revenue per service automatically.

---

## Monthly check (5 min)

Open the `Summary` tab. Look at **Total Leads by Source**'s real winner. If 80% of paying jobs came from Nextdoor and zero from Craigslist, stop spending Monday time on Craigslist and put it into Nextdoor + Google. Let the spreadsheet decide, not guesswork.
