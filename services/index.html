01 / 04 :: fire safety & efficiency

Dryer Vent Cleaning

Lint buildup inside a dryer vent is a leading cause of house fires, and a blocked vent also means longer dry times and higher energy bills. We clear the entire vent line, from the dryer inlet to the exterior hood, not just the part you can reach with a vacuum.

Most homes are finished in under an hour. You will see the before and after for yourself, and your dryer goes back to drying a load in a single cycle.

Flat rate: $100most single-family homes · firm quote first

Service reduces fire risk by removing accessible lint buildup. We are not responsible for pre-existing ductwork defects, code violations, inaccessible duct sections, or causes unrelated to the vent cleaned.
