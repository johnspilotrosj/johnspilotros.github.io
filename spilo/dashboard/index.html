Backup

A full backup is every contact, every touch, every template, every sequence, and every parcel in one file. Keep it somewhere that is not this laptop. Restoring merges by id, so it adds and updates but never wipes what is already there.

Templates

Merge fields you can drop into any of these: {firstName} {street} {area} {price} {value}. On a contact, one tap fills them in and copies the result to your clipboard. Nothing sends by itself.

Sequences

The schedule behind each segment, as plain JSON you can edit. A step is dayOffset counted from the day they signed in, a channel, a templateId, and a label. Steps come due in the Today tab. You send every one of them by hand.

Sign in sheets
