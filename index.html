+--[ 02 :: why vents matter ]--+

Lint is fuel. A clean vent is cheap insurance.

Every load of laundry pushes lint past the trap and into the vent line. Over time it packs the duct, traps heat and makes your dryer work harder. Failure to clean is the leading cause of home clothes dryer fires.

2,900home dryer fires reported in the U.S. each year
$35Min property loss from those fires annually
34%are caused by failure to clean the dryer

source: U.S. Fire Administration estimates

> 6 warning signs your dryer vent is clogged

  • Clothes need more than one cycle to dry
  • The dryer or the laundry is hot to the touch
  • You notice a burning or musty smell while it runs
  • The outside vent flap barely opens
  • Lint collects around the exterior hood or behind the dryer
  • It has been more than a year since the last cleaning

Questions, answered

Straight answers about pricing, timing and what to expect. Still not sure? Ask us directly and we will get back to you the same day.

Ask a question
How much does dryer vent cleaning cost?

Dryer vent cleaning starts at a flat $100. You get a firm price before we begin, and if conditions on site differ from what was described we tell you before any work starts.

How long does a dryer vent cleaning take?

Most homes take under an hour. We brush the full vent line, flush the lint trap and housing, and inspect the exhaust duct.

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

A good rule is at least once a year, and sooner if clothes take longer to dry, the dryer feels hot, or the outside flap barely opens. Large households and long vent runs fill up faster.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Eagle and serve Boise, Meridian, Star, Nampa, Caldwell and the surrounding Treasure Valley.

Are quotes really free?

Yes. Every quote is free and every job is priced by the job, not by the hour, so the number you approve is the number you pay for the scope described.

What junk can you not take?

We cannot haul hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals, solvents, fuels, batteries or other regulated waste. Furniture, appliances, yard debris and construction junk are all fine.

Do I need to prepare anything before you arrive?

No prep is needed. We bring our own equipment. Please secure pets, point out any fragile or painted surfaces before power washing, and confirm which items are going before junk removal.

What if I am not satisfied with the work?

Contact us within 48 hours of completion and we will return to correct the issue at no additional charge. The guarantee covers the labor we performed.

What are your hours and how fast do you respond?

We work Monday through Saturday, 7 AM to 6 PM, and respond to quote requests the same day. If you need to reschedule, we ask for 24 hours notice.
